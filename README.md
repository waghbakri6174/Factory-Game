# 🔥 Wagh Bakri Factory Escape 🔥

A **terminal-based choice game** written in Python where **one wrong decision = instant GAME OVER**.  
Survive encounters, unlock secret achievements, and decide the fate of the Wagh Bakri factory ☕💣

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

You wake up **alone** inside the **Wagh Bakri factory**.  
No friends. No hope. Just machines… and tea fumes.

Every choice matters.
There are **no second chances**.

---

## ⚠️ Core Rules

- ❌ **One wrong option = GAME OVER**
- ⏹️ Game stops instantly on failure
- ✅ Only one **true path** leads to victory
- 🏆 Hidden achievements exist
- 🎭 Story-driven with characters & sacrifice

---

## 🧠 Gameplay Mechanics

- Pure **text-based input**
- Choose options by typing `1` or `2`
- Global state tracks:
  - Inventory
  - Companions
  - Achievements
  - Story flags

---

## 🧍 Characters You’ll Meet

- **Teddy** – drunk physics teacher powered by chai
- **Man** – muscular guard (do NOT fight)
- **Dose** – optional secret achievement encounter
- **Malik** – your only true ally
- **Lehna** – business opportunity (or betrayal)
- **Wagh** – final boss ☠️

---

## 🏆 Achievements

- **DOSE**  
  Unlock by choosing the correct dialogue with Dose.

---

## 🎵 Special Features

- 🎶 **Duet mechanic** (required for progress)
- 💼 Business system with inventory rewards
- 💣 Tea Bomb (the only viable weapon)
- 😢 Emotional sacrifice ending
- 🔥 TRUE ENDING route

---

## 🏁 Endings

- ❌ Multiple instant-death endings
- 🔥 **TRUE ENDING** (only one correct path)
- 🕊️ Malik’s fate changes the final narrative
- 🏆 Secret achievement acknowledgment
